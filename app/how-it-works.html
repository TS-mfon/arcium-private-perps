OpenRiskSettle

How it works

Positions become visible only where the market actually needs visibility.

The product separates private trade composition from public account creation, then pulls the created Solana accounts back into the interface so the trader can inspect the real state immediately.

Operating logic

  1. The trader opens a position and writes the position PDA on-chain.
  2. Risk and settlement steps emit real receipts instead of fake frontend state.
  3. The local workspace stores the private thesis and maps it to the explorer path.

Why the split matters

  1. Readable position details invite copy-trading and liquidation targeting.
  2. Local drafts preserve the private structure of the trade.
  3. Explorer-linked receipts still give operators and users a verifiable trail.
